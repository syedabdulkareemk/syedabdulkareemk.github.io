Delete Account Request

JUDO – Survival (Xilver Studios)

Purpose: This page explains how to request deletion of your JUDO – Survival account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion, email us at:

  • Email: syedabdulkareemk@gmail.com
  • Subject line: “JUDO – Survival — Delete My Account”

In your email, please include the following so we can verify and locate your account:

  • Your in-game username / display name
  • Your User ID (if visible in the app settings/profile)
  • The sign-in method used (for example: Google / email / guest)
  • Any additional details that help identify your account (optional)

We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and may ask for additional details to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When we process an approved deletion request, we will delete (or de-identify where applicable) data associated with your account, such as:

  • Account identifiers (User ID)
  • Username / display name
  • Profile avatar selection
  • Gameplay progress (where linked to your account)
  • Match history and gameplay statistics (where linked to your account)
  • Rankings/leaderboard entries (where linked to your account)
  • In-game currency balances (where linked to your account)

What Data May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period or in an aggregated/anonymized form for the following reasons:

  • Security and fraud prevention: to detect abuse, cheating, and protect users.
  • Legal compliance: if we are required to keep certain records.
  • Analytics and crash reports: may be stored by third-party services in aggregated or device-based form.

Retention Period

We aim to complete account deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe. If any data must be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons, it will be kept only as long as necessary and then deleted or anonymized.

Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services (for example: Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, AdMob, and potentially Unity Ads). These providers may retain certain data under their own privacy policies.

Need Help?

If you have questions about this process, contact us at syedabdulkareemk@gmail.com.
